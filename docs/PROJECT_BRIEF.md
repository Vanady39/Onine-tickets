# Project 3 — Online Tickets (original high-level requirements)

Source: "High-level requirements for software systems for the 'Software Systems Architecture'
course", item 3.

> An online ticket booking and sales system. Sale of tickets for concerts, cinema, theatre and
> exhibitions. The system allows users to view the programme of events and information about each
> one. For each event, the system provides a schedule specifying the date, time, venue and ticket
> prices. Consequently, tickets for the cinema, theatre and concerts may vary in price, depending on
> the seat in the auditorium. Additional services may be added to exhibition tickets for example, a
> business lunch or attendance at masterclasses/presentations.
>
> The system must track customers' ticket purchase history and their various statuses. For regular
> customers, it is possible to purchase up to three tickets at a time for a single event, paying in
> cash at the box office or by bank card. Loyal customers (those who have made a purchase more than
> three times) can book tickets for several events, similar to a season ticket for a concert hall.
>
> The ticket-purchasing process involves selecting an event, choosing a seat in the auditorium (if
> applicable), selecting a payment method, choosing a delivery option and confirming the purchase.
> Once confirmed, the system processes the ticket purchase transaction and, if delivery has been
> selected, sends a delivery request to the courier service.
>
> When booking a ticket, the customer is issued with a booking code, which guarantees they can
> purchase the tickets one day before the event.
